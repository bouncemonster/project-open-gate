## 7. Interpretation

The detector achieves 97.5% balanced accuracy on a class-balanced holdout
with 50% trivial baseline. This is a genuine signal.

**However**: detecting "quantized data" or "discrete generators" is NOT
equivalent to detecting "simulated reality" (§30). The correct interpretation
is: **known computational artifact detection works**.

The high accuracy (97.5%) warrants adversarial testing in V5.2 to determine
whether the detector exploits superficial statistical differences or
genuine computational signatures.
